# L²CU

L²CU (LDraw Linux Command Line Utility) is an open-source toolkit
designed to streamline the creation, management and processing of
digital LEGO® models. Built for Linux, it brings together model
rendering, 3D format conversion, building instruction generation and
powerful batch-editing capabilities in one versatile utility.

Developed to bridge gaps in existing LEGO® design workflows, L²CU
automates repetitive tasks and makes managing complex models and
assemblies easier. From preparing models for visualization and 3D
applications to refining entire libraries of parts and assemblies, it
brings greater flexibility and efficiency to digital LEGO® creation.
